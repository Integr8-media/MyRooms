### Full description [2825 / 4000]

```text
MyRooms is made for gaming centers that rent PC desks and PlayStation rooms by the hour. See what is free and what is playing, run every session from start to payment, take bookings, and know your income at the end of the day.

LIVE FLOOR
Every station on one screen, colour-coded: free, playing, ending soon, over time, reserved soon, or in maintenance. Each card shows the timer, players and the running cost.

SESSIONS
- Open sessions that run until you stop them, or fixed time with a duration or an hour package.
- Add time, change the number of players, or move the group to another station.
- Alerts 5 minutes before the end, at the end, and when a session runs over.

PRICING THAT FITS YOUR CENTER
- Hourly prices per station type, for example PS5 Room, VIP PC or Standard PC.
- Different prices for peak hours, weekends and the number of players.
- Hour packages, such as 3 hours for a fixed price.
- Billing by the minute, and a price preview to check any session before a customer asks.

CHECKOUT AND PAYMENTS
- A clear bill: time, snacks and drinks, discount and total.
- Record Cash, Visa or Instapay, and split one bill across methods.
- Discounts, refunds and voids are for owners and managers only, with a reason, and every one is recorded with the staff member's name.
- Share or print a receipt.

RESERVATIONS AND CALENDAR
- A timeline of all stations. Tap an empty slot to book, drag to move or resize.
- Check customers in when they arrive, or mark a no-show.
- No double booking, even when two devices book at the same time.

REPORTS
- Income for today, this week, this month or any range, by payment method, branch and station type.
- Net profit after expenses, station usage, busiest hours, top customers and snack sales.
- Compare with the previous period and between branches. Export to CSV or PDF.

OPTIONAL MODULES
Switch on only what you need: customers with a default discount, snacks and drinks with stock, and expenses.

BRANCHES AND STAFF
- Several branches in one organization.
- Owner, Manager and Cashier roles. Cashiers can't change prices and see only the money they took on their own shift.
- Invite staff by email.

KEEPS WORKING WITHOUT INTERNET
If the connection drops, keep working. Everything is saved on the device and syncs when the internet is back. A sync badge always shows the state, and nothing that the server refuses disappears without a notice.

ARABIC AND ENGLISH
Full Arabic (right to left) and English. Amounts in Egyptian pounds (EGP).

ONE ACCOUNT, EVERY DEVICE
Use MyRooms on Android, iPhone and iPad, in the browser, and on Windows. Every device sees the same live data.

Good to know: MyRooms records how a customer paid. It does not charge cards or send Instapay requests. An account is needed to sign in; the owner creates the organization and invites the staff.
```
