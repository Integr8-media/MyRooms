MyRooms user guide

MyRooms runs a gaming center: PC desks and PlayStation rooms rented by the hour. This guide is for owners, managers and cashiers. Button and screen names are shown in bold, exactly as the app shows them.

1. Getting started

Create your account

  1. Open the app and tap New here? Create an account.
  2. Enter your full name, email and a password, then tap Create account.
  3. Open the confirmation email and tap the link. Then come back and Sign in.

Staff: sign up with the same email address your manager invited. If you forget your password, tap Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen.

The first sign-in on each phone or computer needs internet. After that the app also works offline.

Create your organization and quick setup

Your organization is your gaming-center business. It can have several branches (locations).

  1. After the first sign-in, under Create your organization, enter the organization name and the name of your first branch. Tap Create organization.
  2. Quick setup opens. Enter how many PlayStation rooms and how many PC desks you have, and the hourly price of each.
  3. The app creates a station type for each, a base hourly price, and the stations themselves (numbered). You can rename them and add more prices later. You can also skip quick setup and add everything yourself in Settings.

Prices are per station per hour (a whole room, not per player) and charged per minute: 30 minutes cost half the hourly price.

Setup checklist

Owners see a Setup checklist card on the live floor. It links to the things to do before the first customer:

  • Prices: peak hours, weekends, player counts, hour packages.
  • Stations: names, extra stations, maintenance.
  • Staff: invite your managers and cashiers.
  • Modules: switch Customers, Snacks and Expenses on or off.

When you are done, you can hide the card.

Invite your staff

  1. Go to Settings › Staff and tap Invite.
  2. Enter their email, choose a Role and their Branches.
  3. Tap Send invitation. This needs internet.

The person gets an email. They create an account with that email (or sign in if they already have one), and tap Join under Invitations for you. A manager or cashier with no branch can't work anywhere: assign at least one branch.

2. Daily work for staff

The live floor

The home screen, Live floor, shows every station in the current branch. Each card shows the timer, players and running cost. The colour tells you the status:

  • Free (green): tap to start a session.
  • Playing (blue-violet): a session is running.
  • Ending soon (orange): 5 minutes or less left on a fixed-time session.
  • Over time (red): the planned time has passed.
  • Reserved soon (light blue): a reservation starts within 30 minutes. Tap to check the customer in.
  • Maintenance (grey): out of service, can't be started or booked.

The header has the branch name (tap it to switch branch if you work in several), the sync badge, Calendar, Reports (cashiers: My shift) and Settings. The floor also lists Arrivals (reservations due soon or late) and Waiting for payment (ended sessions not fully paid).

Start a session

  1. Tap a Free station.
  2. Choose Open (the timer runs until you end it) or Fixed time (choose How long: a quick duration, Other for minutes, or a package).
  3. Set the number of players.
  4. Optional: choose a Customer and add a note. The screen shows the current rate or the estimated price.
  5. Tap Start session.

If a reservation is coming on that station, the app warns you so a walk-in doesn't block it.

During a session

Tap a running station to open its session. From there:

  • Add time: adds to the planned end of a fixed-time session.
  • Change players: use the player buttons, then Change to … players. The new count is priced from now on.
  • Move to another station: pick a free station. A session with a package can only move to a station of the same type.
  • Snacks & drinks: add items (see below).

The screen shows the Bill so far (estimate) and the history of changes.

End a session and take payment

  1. On the session, tap End session, then End now.
  2. Checkout opens with the bill: time, snacks, discount, Total, Paid and Still to pay.
  3. Under Take payment, choose Cash, Visa or Instapay. The amount is filled in with what is due.
  4. For cash, you can enter Cash received (optional) to see the change. For Visa or Instapay, you can enter the slip or transfer number as Reference (optional).
  5. Tap the record button (Record … by …).

Split payment: enter less than the full amount, record it, then record the rest with another method. The app doesn't charge cards or send Instapay requests: it only records how the customer paid.

Payments are never edited or deleted. If a payment was wrong, an owner or manager makes a Refund and you record it again correctly.

If you leave checkout before the customer pays, the session stays in Waiting for payment on the floor.

Receipts

On checkout, tap Print or share receipt. On a phone you get a PDF to share (for example on WhatsApp). On a computer the print window opens; choose a printer or Save as PDF. The receipt number appears after the device syncs.

Snacks and drinks

When the Snacks module is on, the session and checkout screens have a Snacks & drinks card. Pick a product, set the quantity, tap Add. Items appear on the bill. To remove one, tap Remove; it goes back into stock. After the session ends, only owners and managers can remove items, and only before any payment.

If the stock says a product is out, you can still sell it; the owner or manager corrects the count later.

Customers

When the Customers module is on, you can link a customer when starting a session, booking, or on the session screen. Search by phone or name. If nobody matches, tap Add as a new customer. If the phone number already belongs to someone, the app tells you and lets you choose that customer instead.

The Customers screen (link on the floor) shows each customer's visits and total paid. A customer can have a Default discount (%), set by an owner or manager; it is taken off the bill automatically when the session ends.

Reservations and the calendar

Tap Calendar on the floor. Stations are rows, time runs along the day.

  • Book: tap an empty time. Fill in the station, date, start time, length, players, customer name and phone (optional), then tap Book. You can also tap New reservation.
  • Move or resize: tap a reservation to select it, then drag it to another time or station, or drag its end to change the length. Tap it again to open it.
  • Day and Week views: week view is on larger screens (tablet, computer).
  • Check in: when the customer arrives, tap the station on the floor (or the reservation in Arrivals) and tap Check in now. The timer starts and the session ends at the booked time; you can add time later. If the booked station is busy, use Check in on another station.
  • No-show: 15 minutes after the start time you can tap Mark as no-show.
  • Cancel: open the reservation and tap Cancel reservation.

The app never lets two reservations take the same station at the same time. If two offline devices book the same slot, the server keeps the first and the other device shows it under Not saved (see Working offline).

Session alerts

For fixed-time sessions the app alerts you 5 minutes before the end, at the end, and 15 minutes over. Allow notifications when asked. On a computer (web or Windows app) the app must be open. Each device can turn alerts off in Settings › Account › Session alerts.

3. For owners and managers

Prices, packages and price preview

Owners set prices in Settings › Station types & prices.

  • Station type: a group of stations with the same price, e.g. "PS5 Room" or "VIP PC", with a maximum number of players.
  • Hourly prices: start with a base price for all days and times. Tap Add price for peak hours, weekends or player counts (e.g. 4 players). When several prices match, the highest Priority wins (base 0, peak or weekend 10).
  • Hour packages: a fixed price for a number of hours, e.g. 3 hours for 150 EGP. Time after the package is charged at the hourly price.
  • Price preview (in Settings): pick a type, players, start and end, and see the price line by line. Use it to check your prices.

Bills already made keep their amounts when you change prices.

Stations and maintenance

In Settings › Stations (current branch), tap Add stations to add several at once; they are numbered after the name. Turn on Maintenance when a station is out of service: it can't be started or booked. Turn off In use to remove a station from the floor; its history is kept.

Discounts, refunds and voids

  • Discount: on checkout, Give a discount as an amount or a percent, with an optional reason. Only before any payment is taken.
  • Refund: tap Refund, enter the amount, method and a reason (required). You can't refund more than was paid.
  • Void bill: for a bill that should not count at all. A reason is required and it can't be undone. Refund any money taken first.

Every discount, refund and void is recorded with the name of who did it.

Reports and export

Tap Reports on the floor. Choose a period and a branch:

  • Today, This week (weeks start on Saturday), This month or Custom.
  • Overview: income (payments minus refunds) by payment method, branch, station type and day, compared with the previous period. With Expenses on: Net profit.
  • Usage: how much station time was used, and Busiest hours.
  • Customers: top customers. Snacks & drinks: what sold.
  • Export CSV (opens in Excel) or Export PDF.

Income counts on the day the money changed hands. Void bills don't count. Payments taken offline on other devices appear once they sync.

Cashiers see My shift: only the money they took today. Check the Cash line against the drawer.

Expenses

Tap Expenses on the floor, then Add expense: category, amount, how it was paid, date and a note. Expenses are per branch. Cashiers don't see them.

Products and stock

Tap Snacks & stock on the floor. Owners add products (Add product) with a price and can turn on Count stock with a low-stock warning. Products are never deleted; use Stop selling. Owners and managers use Restock when goods arrive and Count to correct the stock after counting the shelf.

Stock in a branch = restocks and corrections minus everything sold in that branch.

Optional modules

In Settings › Organization › Optional modules, the owner switches Customers, Snacks & drinks (with stock) and Expenses on or off. Turning a module off hides it everywhere. Its data is kept, and snacks already on a bill still show.

Branches

Owners add locations in Settings › Branches › Add branch. Branches are never deleted, only closed (turn off Open); their history is kept. Each device works in one branch at a time: switch it from the branch name at the top of the floor.

4. Working offline

If the internet drops, keep working. Sessions, payments, reservations, snacks, customers, expenses and reports all work on the device. Changes are saved on the device and upload when the internet is back.

Needs internet: the first sign-in on a device, creating an organization, inviting staff, joining an invitation, and deleting your account.

The sync badge on the floor shows the state. Tap it to open the Sync page.

Badge Meaning
Up to date Everything on this device is saved on the server.
Syncing… / Connecting… Sending and receiving changes, or trying to reach the server.
Offline No connection. Keep working; changes upload later.
Not uploading The server is reachable but isn't taking the changes yet. Nothing is lost; they are retried.
Sync problem This device can't receive updates. Tap Sync now; if it continues, tell the owner.

On the Sync page, Sync now retries at once. The page also shows the last sync, the changes waiting to upload and the device clock.

The "Not saved" list

Sometimes the server refuses a change made offline, usually because another device got there first (for example, two devices booked the same station). The change then appears on the floor under Not saved, with the reason. Nothing disappears silently.

  • For a reservation, tap Book again to book it at another time or station.
  • For a payment or refund, the amount and method are shown: record it again on the right bill.
  • When you have dealt with an entry, tap Dismiss.
While changes are waiting to upload: don't sign out and don't clear the browser data, or they are lost. On computers, use the Windows app or install the web app so the browser keeps the data.

Keep the device clock right. Sessions, payments and bills use the device's clock. Turn on automatic date and time. The Sync page warns you if the clock is more than 2 minutes off.

5. Windows app updates

The Windows app updates itself. When a new version is ready, it shows Version … is available. Tap Install and restart. It is best to do this when the sync badge says Up to date and no session is being checked out. Phones update through Google Play or the App Store; the web app updates when you reload the page.

6. Deleting your account

Go to Settings › Account › Delete my account. This needs internet and can't be undone. The app explains what will be deleted. Type the word it asks for, then confirm.

  • Your sign-in is deleted, and your name and email are removed from the organization.
  • Bills, payments and other business records you made stay with the organization, but no longer show your name or email.
  • If you are the only owner and the organization has other staff, first make someone else owner (Settings › Staff › choose the person › Role).
  • If you are the only member of the organization, the organization and all its data (stations, sessions, bills, payments, customers…) are deleted too. Export your reports first if you want a copy.

Before deleting, wait until the sync badge says Up to date. You can also delete your account from the web app. See the privacy policy.

7. Troubleshooting

I didn't get the confirmation or invitation email.

Check the spam folder and wait a few minutes. Make sure you used the exact email address that was invited. Still nothing: ask the owner to withdraw and send the invitation again.

A station can't be started.

It may be in Maintenance, someone may already be playing on it (check from another device after sync), or no price is set for this time. Ask the owner to add a base price in Station types & prices.

I can't give a discount.

Only owners and managers can, and only before any payment. If money was already taken, refund it first, then give the discount.

I recorded the wrong payment method or amount.

Payments can't be edited. An owner or manager makes a Refund with a reason, then you record the payment again correctly.

Alerts don't appear.

Allow notifications for the app in the phone or browser settings, and check Settings › Account › Session alerts. On a computer the app must be open. Station cards still change colour when time runs out.

The badge says "Not uploading" for a long time.

Nothing is lost: the changes stay on the device and are retried. Don't sign out. Open the Sync page and tap Sync now. If it continues, contact the owner or support.

A reservation or payment I made disappeared.

Look at the Not saved list on the floor. It shows what the server refused and why. Book again or record the payment again.

A staff member can't see a branch or the stations.

Check Settings › Staff: they need at least one branch. Then they switch to that branch at the top of the floor.

Times or amounts look wrong.

Check the device clock (Sync page). Check the time zone in Settings › Organization (for example Africa/Cairo). Bill amounts on the device are estimates until the server confirms them after sync.
